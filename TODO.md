## 1.2.0 production showcase (2026-10-19)

The owner authorized promotion to `https://alvenx.com/ui/` after implementation and self-review. This release adds the canonical glass header, layered blue/indigo/violet background, gradient “Glass that follows your hand.” headline, and a Progress demonstration starting at 50%. Numeric progress editing uses 0–100 with one-percent display steps; continuous gesture positions retain their unrounded input. Material defaults and ranges remain the kit's existing contract, including opacity 0.3 and both blur ranges 0–6 px.

The reusable `.alvenx-design` scope suppresses the platform tap highlight with `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent`. It preserves the authored press/deformation response, contour keyboard focus and native field selection. The reported grey switch pill and range rectangle match Apple's documented native tap overlay; the actual disappearance must still be checked on the owner's iPad/iPhone.

Release evidence and remaining work are maintained in the [1.2.0 release record](docs/releases/1.2.0/README.md). Historical production and review versions remain archived. Continue physical iPhone 15 Pro / iPad Air M4 checks for the first tap, touch and Combo Touch drag, overdrag/re-grab, scroll ownership and the platform overlay. Desktop emulation does not close those acceptance items. The public source remains all rights reserved / UNLICENSED.

1.1.1 hover optimization keeps the existing contour, material settings, normal resolution, native PNG compression and 60Hz work budget. The blur implementation uses contiguous access, bounded column sums and zero-support regions with floating-point tail preservation. Exact normal-map comparisons and settled-state screenshots accompany the measured production-build comparison; transient physics and continuous pointer precision are not quantized to save work.
