Derived with fonttools[woff] 4.60.1; width axis at its source default,
weight axis fixed at 400, 500 or 600. Internal family renamed to
AlvenX Optical Latin to distinguish the derived faces from the original.
Used only for optical-source typography to avoid the observed Windows
WebKit Canvas variable-weight rasterization defect. Original font and
native product typography remain available. Rebuild: scripts/build-optical-fonts.py.
