# R7 process and verification boundaries

The owner reported two visibly different mothers during/following growth, requested a real open/close toggle and brighter/paler volume, then reported a white outer edge on phones. The active change remains the separate review; accepted geometry is unchanged.

The first R7 source `188693af02c572126c326af9eb95625b0a3256cf` was deployed and tested, but final screenshot inspection still found a thin lower white seam at 320 CSS px / DPR 3. Its original entry and manifest are retained in the R7 archive. This final R7 follow-up `82de4e4cae458dd716ef3462c9c94192d1114265` removes the contact-shadow SourceAlpha cutout in both static and live views. The soft shadow now continues beneath the translucent material; geometry and renderer textures did not change. A pixel-ridge regression reproduces the previous seam and checks both final local and online screenshots. The earlier CSS-only rim assertion was insufficient for this visual bug.

The material changed from phase-specific analytic/profile height models to one displayed-contour profile. Its parser now handles two closed subpaths. Duplicate native/reference rims and doubled alpha attenuation at the clipped boundary were removed. The broad internal environment reflection remains; the palette was lifted and modestly saturated. First-frame PNGs were regenerated from this renderer, and the initial/live contact shadow now matches.

A held touch crossing hydration exposed a real double activation: pointer release opened, then a synthesized click toggled closed. The handoff now consumes only that compatibility click and releases interception on a new pointerdown/key, timeout, or cleanup. The regression verifies the immediate second and third genuine gestures.

The original reversal probe compared values around automation round trips, so ordinary animation progress caused a false failure. The final probe captures the pre-click value, then reads in a window-bubble microtask after React commits. It also asserts the new target in that same snapshot, preventing an early pre-commit read from passing vacuously. Queued animation frames can also commit during a native click. The continuity probe therefore holds only the browser presentation RAF for 32 ms to drain prior work, performs the native click, reads the committed target/growth, then resumes RAF and verifies actual settling. This isolates handler continuity at 0.0001 tolerance without treating normal frame progress as a discontinuity. Normal live open/close, gesture handoff and responsive interaction checks remain unpaused. Both final local and hosted suites pass.

Intermediate development included a shader syntax typo during cleanup (repaired before final build), a stale archive-status assertion in the publication checker (updated to the R7 archive classification without weakening hash checks), and timeouts in the reused browser session. The full responsive/material suite passed in fresh isolated browser contexts without relaxing its timeout. Temporary diagnostics are not presented as final product acceptance.

`material-lifecycle-before.png` and `material-lifecycle.png` are deliberately uncomposited renderer fixtures, useful for phase comparison, not screenshots of the live DOM. Actual local/hosted phone, tablet and desktop captures are separate. The retained local geometry tests passed 392 assertions; contract commands passed 28 contract and 410 library geometry assertions. TypeScript/docs, separate Vite build/prerender, full source CI and website Linux CI passed.

The online suite repeats the responsive interactions, full-lifecycle profile alignment, startup/no-JS/failure handling, held gesture handoff, and touch/DPR/keyboard/reduced-motion toggles. Desktop Chromium emulation does not certify physical iPhone/iPad Safari, Combo Touch, power use or stable FPS. First-frame visibility remains distinct from live readiness; the existing no-KHR synchronous compatibility wait and temporarily flatter glass during forced compilation are retained boundaries. No zero-delay claim is made.
